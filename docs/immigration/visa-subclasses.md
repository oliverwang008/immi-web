# Australian Skilled & Employer-Sponsored Visas — Reference

## How to read this doc

- **VAC (base)** = the first-instalment Visa Application Charge for the *main/primary applicant only*. Additional applicants (partner, children) and a possible "second instalment" (e.g. for applicants without functional English) are extra and are **not** included here.
- **Processing times**: The authoritative, monthly-updated figures live in the Department's [Global visa processing times](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-processing-times/global-visa-processing-times) tool, which reports the time taken to finalise the 50% (median) and 90% of applications. That tool is JavaScript-rendered and could not be scraped programmatically for this doc, so the processing-time ranges below are **indicative only**, drawn from recent migration-industry reporting, and **must be re-verified against the official tool**. For an employer-sponsored tracker, query the tool per subclass+stream because times vary widely by stream.
- **Income thresholds** (CSIT/SSIT/TSMIT) are indexed annually on 1 July. The figures below are the values effective **1 July 2025**.

## Key FY2025–26 figures at a glance

| Item | Value (effective) | As of |
|---|---|---|
| Core Skills Income Threshold (CSIT) | **$76,515** (up from $73,150) | 1 Jul 2025 |
| Specialist Skills Income Threshold (SSIT) | **$141,210** (up from $135,000) | 1 Jul 2025 |
| Temporary Skilled Migration Income Threshold (TSMIT) — legacy 482 (TSS), 494, 186 TRT | **$76,515** (up from $73,150) | 1 Jul 2025 |
| Specialist Skills "high earner" cap (no AMSR check needed above this) | **$250,000** | current |

Thresholds apply to nomination/visa applications lodged **on or after** 1 July 2025 and are **not** applied retrospectively to applications lodged earlier.

## Subclass 189 — Skilled Independent visa

**Purpose:** Permanent visa for invited skilled workers (and a separate New Zealand stream) who are **not** sponsored by an employer, state/territory or family member. Lets you live and work anywhere in Australia permanently.

**Streams:**
- **Points-tested stream** — the main skilled-migration stream (requires an invitation).
- **New Zealand stream** — for certain long-term-resident NZ citizens. *(Note: largely superseded since the July 2023 direct citizenship pathway for NZ Special Category visa holders; treat as low-volume. Verify current status.)*

**Key requirements (points-tested):**
- Submit an Expression of Interest (EOI) in SkillSelect and **be invited to apply**.
- Under 45 years old at time of invitation (limited exemptions).
- A suitable **skills assessment** for an occupation on the relevant eligible list.
- Satisfy the **points test** (minimum 65 points to be eligible; actual invitation cut-offs are typically much higher and demand-driven).
- **Competent English** minimum (higher English scores earn points).
- Health and character requirements; Australian Values Statement.

**VAC (base):** $4,910 (as of 1 Jul 2025).

**Indicative processing time:** Highly invitation-driven. Once invited and lodged, commonly reported around ~5–9 months for many applicants, but verify per the official tool. 189 invitation volumes have been low in recent rounds, so the practical wait is dominated by time spent in the EOI pool.

**Recent changes / notes:** Invitation numbers for 189 have been constrained under recent Migration Program planning levels, with priority shifting toward employer-sponsored and state-nominated pathways. Points test and lists unchanged structurally, but the eligible occupation lists were consolidated under the new Core Skills Occupation List (CSOL) framework introduced in Dec 2024 — confirm which list applies to 189.

## Subclass 190 — Skilled Nominated visa

**Purpose:** Permanent visa for invited skilled workers **nominated by an Australian state or territory government**. Live and work permanently anywhere in Australia (though nominating states usually expect you to settle in their state — a commitment, not a visa condition).

**Streams:** Single points-tested stream (requires state/territory nomination + invitation).

**Key requirements:**
- Submit an EOI **and obtain state/territory nomination**, then **be invited to apply**.
- Under 45 at invitation (limited exemptions).
- Suitable **skills assessment** for a nominated occupation on the relevant state list.
- Satisfy the **points test** (65 minimum; +5 points awarded for state nomination).
- **Competent English** minimum; health; character; Australian Values Statement.
- 60 calendar days to lodge after invitation (non-extendable).

**VAC (base):** $4,910 (as of 1 Jul 2025).

**Indicative processing time:** Two-stage — state nomination first (weeks to a few months, varies by state), then DHA processing commonly reported ~6–12 months. Verify per official tool.

**Recent changes / notes:** State/territory allocations for 190 (and 491) have been tight and many programs open/close in short windows or remain paused pending each financial year's allocation release. Occupation eligibility now flows from the CSOL framework plus each state's own list.

## Subclass 491 — Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) — and PR via Subclass 191

### Subclass 491 (provisional, 5 years)

**Purpose:** 5-year **provisional** visa for skilled workers who commit to living and working in **regional Australia**. Pathway to permanent residence via subclass 191.

**Streams / pathways:**
- **State or territory nominated** stream.
- **Family sponsored** stream (sponsored by an eligible relative living in a designated regional area).

**Key requirements:**
- EOI + **either** state/territory nomination **or** eligible family sponsorship, then **be invited to apply**.
- Under 45 at invitation (limited exemptions).
- Suitable **skills assessment**; satisfy the **points test** (65 minimum; +15 points for state nomination or eligible family sponsorship).
- **Competent English** minimum; health; character; Australian Values Statement.
- **Visa condition 8579**: must live, work and study **only** in a designated regional area while holding the visa. (All of Australia except Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane is "designated regional" for this purpose — verify the current postcode list.)

**VAC (base):** $4,910 (as of 1 Jul 2025).

**Indicative processing time:** Commonly reported ~8–14 months after lodgement; verify per official tool. Plus prior state nomination/family sponsorship time.

### Subclass 191 — Permanent Residence (Skilled Regional)

**Purpose:** The **permanent** outcome for 491 (and 494) holders who have lived and worked in regional Australia.

**Key requirements (per current official page, accessed 2026-06-28):**
- Hold an **eligible regional provisional visa** (491 or 494) and have held it for **at least 3 years** before applying.
- **Complied with the conditions** of the eligible visa (notably **condition 8579** for 491 holders) for the whole period held.
- Provide **ATO notices of assessment for 3 income years** out of the 5 years of the eligible visa as evidence of work/residence.
- **Income requirement:** The current official 191 page states **"There is no minimum income requirement."** ⚠️ This differs from earlier rules (which required minimum taxable income, historically tied to ~$53,900). Treat the no-minimum wording as current per the official page but **flag for verification**, since the developer's tracker should not hard-code a minimum income gate without confirming.
- Lived, worked and studied in a **designated regional area** during the qualifying period.

**Recent changes / notes:** The 491→191 pathway requires three years on the provisional visa. The apparent removal of a hard minimum-income threshold (with the requirement reframed as "provide 3 years of ATO notices of assessment") is the most consequential nuance to verify before building income-validation logic.

## Subclass 482 — Skills in Demand (SID) visa  ⚠️ replaced TSS in Dec 2024

**Major change:** On **7 December 2024**, the **Skills in Demand (SID)** visa replaced the former **Temporary Skill Shortage (TSS)** visa. It kept the **subclass number 482** but restructured the streams and rules. The old TSS short-term / medium-term / labour-agreement structure is gone.

**Purpose:** Temporary visa allowing an approved employer/sponsor to fill a position they cannot fill with an Australian worker. Now a clearer pathway to PR (via 186).

**Three current streams:**

| Stream | Who it's for | Salary test | Occupation basis | Max stay |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Core Skills** | Mainstream skilled workers | Must be paid the **Annual Market Salary Rate (AMSR)** and no less than the **CSIT ($76,515)** | Occupation on the **Core Skills Occupation List (CSOL)** | 4 years (5 for Hong Kong passport holders) |
| **Specialist Skills** | High-paid specialists | Salary ≥ **SSIT ($141,210)**; AMSR also applies (no AMSR check needed if salary ≥ $250,000) | Broad — ANZSCO Major Groups 1, 2, 4, 5, 6 (excludes Major Groups 3, 7, 8); no fixed occupation list | 4 years (5 for HK) |
| **Labour Agreement** | Workers nominated under a labour agreement | Per the labour agreement / AMSR | Per the labour agreement | 4 years (5 for HK) |

**Key requirements (Core Skills / Specialist Skills):**
- Employer must be an approved **sponsor** and lodge an approved **nomination**.
- At least **1 year of relevant full-time-equivalent work experience** in the nominated occupation or a related field (gained within the last 5 years; part-time/casual can count if equivalent to 12 months full-time). **⚠️ Reduced from 2 years under the old TSS rules.**
- Meet minimum **English language proficiency** (unless exempt).
- Adequate **health insurance**, health, character, "no paying for visa sponsorship", Australian Values Statement.

**VAC (base):** $3,210 (as of 1 Jul 2025) — applies across all three streams (the old tiered TSS short-term vs medium-term VAC split is gone).

**Indicative processing time:** Historically fast for sponsored visas; commonly reported in the ~weeks-to-a-few-months range, varying by stream (Specialist Skills was intended to be priority/fast-tracked). Verify per official tool.

**What changed (TSS → SID), summary:**
- **Three streams** (Core / Specialist / Labour Agreement) replace short-term/medium-term/labour-agreement.
- Uniform **4-year** validity for all streams (was 2 years for TSS short-term).
- **Work experience cut from 2 years to 1 year.**
- New **Core Skills Occupation List (CSOL)** replaces the prior STSOL/MLTSSL lists.
- Clearer **pathway to PR via subclass 186** for all streams; all time on a 482 counts.
- **Worker mobility:** workers get a grace period to find a new sponsor after leaving an employer (aligned with July 2024 changes); sponsors no longer liable to ensure the worker stays in the nominated occupation after employment ends.
- New **employer financial-capacity** expectations (ability to pay AMSR for the visa duration).
- Some announced reforms (e.g. revised labour market testing, reduced upfront employer costs) were **still pending further legislation** as of the latest reporting — verify current LMT rules.

## Subclass 186 — Employer Nomination Scheme (ENS, permanent)

**Purpose:** **Permanent** employer-sponsored visa. Lets an approved employer nominate a skilled worker for permanent residence.

**Three streams:**

| Stream | Core eligibility |
|---|---|
| **Temporary Residence Transition (TRT)** | Held a subclass **482 (or 457)** visa and worked in **eligible sponsored employment for at least 2 years within the 3 years** before applying. Nominated by the employer that last sponsored you. (⚠️ Reduced from the former ~3-year requirement; under SID reforms, time in **any** occupation you were approved to work in can count, and **all** 482 streams can access TRT.) |
| **Direct Entry (DE)** | Under **45**; occupation on the **Core Skills Occupation List (CSOL)**; **at least 3 years** relevant work experience; positive **skills assessment**; competent English. For applicants with little/no prior Australian sponsorship. |
| **Labour Agreement** | Nominated by an employer with a **labour agreement**; terms per that agreement. |

**Common requirements:** Under 45 at application (exemptions apply, e.g. certain TRT high-income/academic cases); competent English; health; character; genuine position; nominated salary at least the AMSR and (where applicable) the relevant income threshold (TSMIT/CSIT — confirm which applies post-reform).

**VAC (base):** $4,910 (as of 1 Jul 2025).

**Indicative processing time:** Commonly reported ~5–12 months, varying by stream (DE often slower than TRT). Verify per official tool.

**Recent changes / notes:** The TRT pathway was made more accessible under the Dec 2024 SID reforms — 2-year (not 3-year) qualifying employment, countable across approved occupations, and open to all 482 stream holders. The DE stream is tied to the CSOL.

## Subclass 494 — Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional (Provisional)

**Status:** **Still current** for new applications as at this research date, but check whether your target states/regions are actively nominating. It is the regional employer-sponsored counterpart to the 482, leading to PR via subclass 191.

**Purpose:** 5-year **provisional** visa for skilled workers sponsored by an employer in a **designated regional area**. Pathway to PR via **subclass 191** (same 3-year provisional pathway as 491).

**Streams:**
- **Employer Sponsored** stream (the main stream).
- **Labour Agreement** stream.

**Key requirements:**
- Approved regional employer **nomination**; position in a designated regional area.
- Under **45** (exemptions apply); positive **skills assessment**; **at least 3 years** relevant work experience; competent English.
- Salary at least the AMSR and no less than **TSMIT ($76,515)**.
- Regional residence condition (live/work/study in designated regional area).

**VAC (base):** ~$4,910 (as of 1 Jul 2025) — confirm via the official pricing table.

**Indicative processing time:** Commonly reported ~6–15 months; verify per official tool.

**Recent changes / notes:** The 494 sits alongside the 491 as the two main regional provisional visas feeding into the 191 PR outcome. Its long-term future has been the subject of policy review under the broader migration strategy — verify it remains open before relying on it.

## Implementation notes for AussieVisa Tracker

- **Don't hard-code processing times.** Pull them live (or refresh regularly) from the official Global visa processing times tool, ideally per subclass *and* stream. The figures above are indicative and unverified.
- **Income thresholds reset every 1 July.** Store CSIT/SSIT/TSMIT as dated, configurable values (current: $76,515 / $141,210 / $76,515 from 1 Jul 2025).
- **VACs reset ~1 July** (CPI indexation). Store as dated values; current base VACs: $4,910 (189/190/491/186/494) and $3,210 (482).
- **Two-stage pipelines:** 190/491 (state nomination → invitation → visa) and 482/186/494 (sponsorship → nomination → visa) need multi-step tracking, not a single processing-time number.
- **Flag the 191 "no minimum income" wording** before building income-validation rules — verify against the official 191 page and current Migration Regulations.
- **CSOL** (Core Skills Occupation List) is now the central occupation list for 482 Core Skills, 186 DE, and feeds skilled lists generally — model occupation eligibility against it (plus state lists for 190/491).
