# Australian State & Territory Nomination Reference (Subclasses 190 & 491)

> Last researched: 2026-06-28. State programs change each program year — verify against each state's official site before relying on them.

A developer reference for **AussieVisa Tracker** covering current (FY2025-26) state and territory nomination settings for the Skilled Nominated visa (subclass 190) and Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) visa (subclass 491).

**Important caveats**
- The 2025-26 program year runs roughly 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026. Many programs opened in the second half of 2025 (the Commonwealth only finalised allocations on **4 November 2025**), and several have **already exhausted** their 2025-26 allocations as of mid-2026. The 2026-27 year is expected to reopen around mid-to-late 2026.
- Allocation figures below come from the Department of Home Affairs allocation page and state announcements. Per-stream splits (e.g. 190 vs 491 within a state) and occupation-list details should be treated as **indicative** and re-verified, as some are sourced via secondary migration-agent summaries.
- Eligibility details (residency periods, English, points) are summarised, not exhaustive. Always check the official criteria document for the relevant program year.

## Federal allocation & program timing (background)

Each program year the Department of Home Affairs sets a **planning level** for the permanent Migration Program, then distributes a pool of **state and territory nomination allocations** across the 8 jurisdictions. An "allocation" is the number of **new primary applicants** a state/territory may nominate that year — it does **not** equal visas granted.

**FY2025-26 totals (as of 4 November 2025):**
- Total state/territory nomination allocations: **20,350** (against a higher Migration Program planning level — i.e. allocations were set well below the notional ceiling).
- Split: **~12,850 for subclass 190** and **~7,500 for subclass 491**.

**Per-jurisdiction allocation (FY2025-26, 190 / 491):**

| Jurisdiction | 190 | 491 | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| NSW | 2,100 | 1,500 | 3,600 |
| VIC | 2,700 | 700 | 3,400 |
| WA | 2,000 | 1,400 | 3,400 |
| QLD | 1,850 | 750 | 2,600 |
| SA | 1,350 | 900 | 2,250 |
| TAS | 1,200 | 650 | 1,850 |
| NT | 850 | 800 | 1,650 |
| ACT | 800 | 800 | 1,600 |

**Typical timing:** Programs usually open after the federal Budget and the new financial year, often **mid-year (July onwards)**. In 2025-26 the formal allocations were unusually late (November 2025), so several states ran interim/limited rounds first. States issue invitations in rounds throughout the year and close once allocations are filled, so the practical "open window" is often only a few months.

## Per-jurisdiction detail

### New South Wales (NSW)
- **Website:** https://www.nsw.gov.au/visas-and-migration/skilled-visas
- **Visas:** 190 and 491.
- **FY2025-26 allocation:** 3,600 total (2,100 × 190; 1,500 × 491).
- **Eligibility (general):** Valid EOI in SkillSelect with NSW selected; under 45; at least Competent English (IELTS 6.0 each band or equivalent); valid skills assessment for a listed occupation.
  - **190 residency:** Either residing in NSW for 6+ continuous months, **or** residing offshore for 6+ continuous months.
  - **491 pathways:** (1) 6+ months continuous employment in nominated occupation with the same regional-NSW employer at TSMIT/CSIT pay; (2) skilled in an occupation on the **NSW Regional Skills List** plus residency (3 months working/living in NSW, or 3 months offshore); (3) recent (within 2 years) bachelor+ from a regional-NSW institution related to the nominated occupation.
- **Occupation list:** NSW selects highest-ranking EOIs within specific ANZSCO unit groups aligned to **priority sectors** (Care & Support Economy, Construction, Digital & Cyber, Net Zero/Energy, Agriculture/Agrifood, Advanced Manufacturing). 491 also references the NSW Regional Skills List.
- **Process:** EOI-driven — NSW invites directly from SkillSelect EOIs (no separate ROI portal for the main 190/491 pathways).
- **Current status flag:** As of ~May 2026, NSW had **issued enough invitations to fill its 190 allocation** for 2025-26; no further 190 invitations until 2026-27. Verify 491 status.

### Victoria (VIC)
- **Website:** https://liveinmelbourne.vic.gov.au/migrate/skilled-migration-visas
- **Visas:** 190 and 491.
- **FY2025-26 allocation:** 3,400 total (2,700 × 190; 700 × 491).
- **Eligibility (general):** Under 45 at nomination; at least Competent English; valid skills assessment in an occupation on the relevant list.
  - **190 location:** Offshore applicants eligible; onshore applicants must be living in Victoria (Victoria does not select onshore ROIs from people living in other states/territories).
  - **491 location:** Offshore applicants eligible; onshore must be in regional Victoria.
- **Occupation list:** Uses Commonwealth occupation lists but **targets priority sectors/occupations** rather than publishing a single bespoke list.
- **Process:** **Registration of Interest (ROI)** required in addition to a SkillSelect EOI; Victoria selects ROIs and issues invitations.
- **Current status flag:** As of mid-2026 the 2025-26 program is **closed to new ROIs**. Existing active ROIs remain valid for the program year; no need to resubmit unless details change.

### Queensland (QLD)
- **Website:** https://migration.qld.gov.au (Migration Queensland)
- **Visas:** 190 and 491.
- **FY2025-26 allocation:** 2,600 total (1,850 × 190; 750 × 491) — a notable increase on prior year.
- **Eligibility (general):** Meet both Home Affairs and Migration Queensland criteria; points test 65+ (incl. state nomination points); positive skills assessment; at least Competent English.
  - **190 work experience:** ≥9 months in nominated occupation in Queensland at 20+ hrs/week.
  - **491 work experience:** ≥6 months at 20+ hrs/week. Casual employment now accepted if 20+ hrs/week in the nominated occupation.
  - **Restriction:** Current/former 491 applicants or holders are **not eligible** for 190 nomination.
- **Occupation list:** Yes — **Queensland Skilled Occupation List (QSOL)** with separate **onshore** and **offshore** versions.
- **Streams:** Skilled workers in QLD; skilled workers offshore; building & construction workers; QLD university graduates; small business owners in regional QLD (491 only).
- **Process:** EOI → **ROI** (a **new ROI must be submitted each program year**) → invitation to apply.

### South Australia (SA)
- **Website:** https://www.migration.sa.gov.au (Move to South Australia)
- **Visas:** 190 and 491.
- **FY2025-26 allocation:** 2,250 total (1,350 × 190; 900 × 491).
- **Eligibility (general):** Minimum 65 points; valid skills assessment; meet stream-specific criteria. Nomination application fee ~AUD 381 (ROI itself is free).
- **Occupation list:** Yes — **South Australia Skilled Occupation List**. For 2025-26 the list has ~395 occupations (≈305 eligible for both 190 & 491; the rest 491-only); SA **cut ~71 onshore occupations** (onshore-eligible occupations dropped from ~464 to ~393). Re-verify exact counts.
- **Streams (4):** SA Graduates; Skilled Employment in South Australia; Outer Regional Skilled Employment; Offshore.
- **Process:** **Onshore** applicants submit an **ROI**; **offshore** applicants do **not** submit an ROI and are invited directly from their SkillSelect EOI.

### Western Australia (WA)
- **Website:** https://migration.wa.gov.au/our-services-support/state-nominated-migration-program (Migration WA)
- **Visas:** 190 and 491 (State Nominated Migration Program — SNMP).
- **FY2025-26 allocation:** 3,400 total (2,000 × 190; 1,400 × 491) — down ~1,600 on prior year.
- **Eligibility (general):** Valid skills assessment; at least 1 year work experience (in last 10 years, Australia or overseas) in the nominated occupation.
  - **190:** Requires a minimum **6-month full-time employment contract in WA** in the nominated (or closely related) occupation.
  - **491:** WA employment-contract requirement does **not** apply.
  - **English exemption:** Passport holders of UK, Canada, NZ, USA or Ireland do not need an English test for WA state nomination.
- **Occupation list:** Yes — **WA Skilled Migration Occupation List (WASMOL) Schedule 1 & Schedule 2**, plus a **Graduate Occupation List (GOL)** for eligible WA VET/higher-ed graduates.
- **Process:** EOI in SkillSelect; WA ranks and invites eligible candidates. Official criteria published in the annual "WA SNMP Criteria" document (July 2025 for this year).

### Tasmania (TAS)
- **Website:** https://www.migration.tas.gov.au (Migration Tasmania); apply via TAS Skilled Migration Gateway (migration-apply.stategrowth.tas.gov.au)
- **Visas:** 190 and 491.
- **FY2025-26 allocation:** ~1,850 total incl. interim allocations (1,200 × 190; 650 × 491).
- **Eligibility (general):** Under 45; skills assessment in nominated occupation; occupation on Tasmania's skilled list; English typically Competent or better. Nominees commit to **live in Tasmania ≥2 years** after nomination approval.
  - **190 pathways:** Tasmanian Skilled Employment (TSE) — faster for "TSE Priority Role" occupations; others need ~15 months' employment in the same ANZSCO 3-digit group. Tasmanian Established Resident (TER) — lived in TAS 3+ years / majority of Australian residence. Overseas applicants for 190 are largely restricted to a targeted **Health or Education Sector Job Offer** pathway.
  - **491:** Provides a pathway to PR via subclass 191.
- **Occupation list:** Yes — Tasmanian skilled lists with **TSE Priority Roles** highlighted.
- **Process:** Create a **Gateway** account → submit **ROI** → receive priority/queue position → if invited, **28 days** to upload documents and pay the service fee to lodge.

### Australian Capital Territory (ACT)
- **Website:** https://www.act.gov.au/migration
- **Visas:** 190 and 491.
- **FY2025-26 allocation:** 1,600 total (800 × 190; 800 × 491 — split evenly).
- **Eligibility (general, Canberra residents):**
  - **190:** Living & working in Canberra ≥6 months; ≥35 hrs/week for the prior 26 weeks; ≥3 years relevant experience in nominated occupation.
  - **491:** Living & working in Canberra ≥3 months; ≥20 hrs/week in each of the prior 13 weeks; ≥1 year relevant experience.
- **Occupation list:** Yes — **ACT Critical Skills List** (reduced from 152 to **105** ANZSCO 4-digit unit groups for 2025-26) plus the **ACT Nominated Migration Program Occupation List**.
- **Process model — the Canberra Matrix:** ACT uses its **own points/ranking system, the Canberra Matrix**, scoring economic contribution and genuine commitment to Canberra. Candidates submit a Matrix; ACT invites the highest-ranked in periodic invitation rounds. Note: **491 invitations are issued before 190**, so 190 candidates typically need a higher Matrix score to be invited.

### Northern Territory (NT)
- **Website:** https://australiasnorthernterritory.com.au/move/migrate-to-work/nt-government-visa-nomination
- **Visas:** 190 and 491.
- **FY2025-26 allocation:** 1,650 total (850 × 190; 800 × 491).
- **Eligibility (general):** Meet Commonwealth GSM requirements first — eligible occupation, valid skills assessment, English, points ≥65; demonstrate strong NT employment outcomes and long-term NT commitment.
  - **Disqualifiers:** Living interstate (you or a migrating dependant) → not eligible; on a student visa with studies not yet completed → not eligible; previously nominated by NT for a 489/491 → not eligible for a 190 nomination.
  - **Residence commitment:** 491 — live & work in NT ≥3 years before PR pathway; 190 — live & work in NT ≥3 years from visa grant.
- **Occupation list:** Yes — NT maintains its own occupation list / stream structure.
- **Process:** Direct **nomination application** via the NT online portal (no SkillSelect-style invitation-only model for the main GSM stream).
- **Current status flag:** NT reported it had **received sufficient applications to fully use its 2025-26 GSM allocation**, and the portal closed to new applications; a reopening date of **24 November 2025** was indicated for onshore and offshore applications. Re-verify current portal status.
